# QEVA roadmap

The roadmap is ordered by structural necessity, not marketing milestones.

## 0 — Stable grammar and map semantics

- exact immutable QEVA record revisions
- typed logical versus historical edges
- explicit uncertainty / verification states
- human-readable and machine-readable views
- portable static release and mirrors

## 1 — Global bibliographic spine

- ingest OpenAlex, Crossref, arXiv, math-specific indexes, OEIS, Wikidata, and public-domain libraries under source-specific rights
- reconcile DOI, arXiv, ISBN, Wikidata, ORCID, author, venue, date, and citation identities
- preserve raw provenance records and deterministic normalized exports

## 2 — Mathematical candidate extraction

Extract candidate:

- definitions
- claims and theorems
- conjectures and open problems
- proofs / proof references
- counterexamples and refutations
- algorithms
- equations and recurrences
- equivalent formulations
- assumptions and prerequisite concepts

Everything produced here remains a candidate until qualified.

## 3 — Qualification and verification

- editorial mathematical review
- source triangulation
- formal proof links where available
- contradiction / correction tracking
- explicit disagreement rather than forced consensus

## 4 — World map

- searchable logical dependency graph across fields
- historical growth timeline
- field branching and cross-field bridges
- multiple foundations / alternative decompositions
- frontier map showing unresolved bottlenecks without ranking popularity as truth

## 5 — Generative sandbox

- executable concept-specific experiments
- parameter sweeps and counterexample search
- automatic conjecture candidates clearly labeled as conjectural
- reusable simulation kernels tied to exact QEVA definitions

## 6 — Preservation succession

- independent institutional mirrors
- periodic complete snapshots
- multiple checksum algorithms
- source-code-independent bootstrap description
- physical/offline editions of the bootstrap and selected foundational maps
- migration drills: reconstruct QEVA from a snapshot without qeva.org
