# QEVA roadmap

The roadmap is ordered by epistemic dependency. More pages are not progress if the archive cannot distinguish source, claim, proof, and qualification.

## 0.4 — shipped in this archive

- Foundation switchboard and full generated logical map.
- Stable object and exact-revision pages.
- Separated history, literature, logical, and editorial layers.
- Bounded Harvester v1 with offline reproducibility test.
- Canonical JSONL/TSV exports and stronger release verification.
- BagIt subset, print core, mirror procedure, and constitution draft.

## 0.5 — corpus pilot

- Run repeatable subsets of OpenAlex, Crossref, arXiv, and Common Crawl URL discovery.
- Add snapshot readers for OpenAlex/Crossref and OAI-PMH checkpoints for arXiv.
- Resolve DOI, arXiv, ORCID, Wikidata, and repository identifiers without merging on titles alone.
- Publish acquisition manifests, rights classifications, failure logs, and benchmark fixtures.
- Recruit one field editorial group and one independent mirror.

**Exit test:** a clean machine can reproduce a 100,000-work pilot corpus and explain every byte's source and rights state.

## 0.6 — mathematical extraction

- Preserve formula structure from TeX/MathML where licensed.
- Segment definitions, results, assumptions, proof references, counterexamples, and open problems.
- Link every candidate back to exact source locators and extraction versions.
- Build disagreement and duplicate queues; never auto-promote candidates to truth.

**Exit test:** reviewers can trace, accept, reject, or split every promoted object without consulting an opaque model state.

## 0.7 — formal bridges

- Import declaration identities and dependency graphs from formal libraries at pinned commits.
- Represent correspondence between informal claims and Lean/Coq/Isabelle/Metamath statements as reviewed mappings.
- Store kernel, compiler, and environment identities alongside certificates.
- Expand executable micro-kernels while keeping their trusted bases explicit.

**Exit test:** a second independent implementation can replay a formal subset from archived source and toolchain descriptions.

## 0.8 — world history graph

- Add works, manuscripts, translations, traditions, people, institutions, dates, and disputed-priority claims.
- Support date intervals, uncertainty, alternative names/scripts, regions, and parallel discovery.
- Establish regional/historical review groups and published correction procedures.

**Exit test:** historical paths are source-backed, disagreement-preserving, and mechanically prevented from becoming proof dependencies.

## 1.0 — federated public institution

- Ratify a public constitution and transparent release process.
- Operate at least five independent mirrors across three legal jurisdictions and three organizational families.
- Complete two annual recovery drills from cold custody.
- Publish signed release witnesses and a practiced succession procedure.

**Exit test:** QEVA remains downloadable, verifiable, and governable after the primary operator is removed from the exercise.

## Century-scale loop

Every generation must migrate media and cryptography, test recovery, preserve prior encodings, translate the recovery core, renew custody, and publish what failed. There is no “store once for a million years” milestone.
