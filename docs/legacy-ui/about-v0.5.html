PROJECT AND BOUNDARY

A mathematical instrument that can outlive its operator.

QEVA is building an explicit, revisioned map of mathematical ideas and a portable environment for testing bounded mechanisms. It is a growing archive, not a claim to contain all mathematics or decide truth automatically.

TWO PLANES

Live services may evolve. Public mathematics must remain recoverable.

A future service may add identity, private work, organizations, compute, and AI agents. Permanent exports contain public artifacts, mathematical records, provenance, history, specifications, and enough software to interpret bounded experiments without that service.

PERMANENT TEST

Can a stranger recover the claims after QEVA disappears?

The domain is a doorway, not the archive. Every release must be downloadable, independently checkable, legally mirrorable, and intelligible without a database, account, package registry, analytics service, or AI provider.
