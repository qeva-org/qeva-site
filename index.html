THE LOAD-BEARING RULE

Published ≠ proved. Cited ≠ fundamental. Simulated ≠ settled.

QEVA stores the difference instead of smoothing it away. Every current object separates a human explanation, exact statement, assumptions, dependencies, verification performed, provenance, and revision history.
