FIRST PRINCIPLES ARCHIVE

What exactly does this mean?

QEVA makes mathematical ideas traceable: plain explanation → exact formulation → assumptions → dependencies → related forms → verification.

What problem does QEVA solve?

Understand

Math often jumps over the assumptions and definitions that make a statement meaningful. QEVA puts the plain and exact versions together.

Trace

A theorem or concept may depend on layers hidden across textbooks and fields. QEVA exposes explicit prerequisite links and pins them to exact revisions.

Compare

The same word can mean different things, and the same structure can have different representations. QEVA records the context instead of flattening the differences.

Preserve

Knowledge should not depend on one website or institution. The archive is downloadable, checkable, mirrorable, and readable as plain structured text.

Permanent rule

QEVA does not ask you to trust a page title. A record should tell you what is being discussed, in which framework, what is assumed, what it depends on, and what checking has actually occurred.

Seed, not completed foundation. The current archive is deliberately small. Its framework labels are not yet recursively reduced to a closed first-principles base. QEVA marks that incompleteness instead of pretending the grand map is already finished.
