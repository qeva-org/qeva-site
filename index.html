One rule

Nothing appears from nowhere. Every record states what it means, what it assumes, what it depends on, how it is represented, and how strongly it is verified.

Human readable

Every object should admit an explanation that a careful non-specialist can follow without sacrificing the exact mathematics.

Machine readable

The canonical archive is plain structured text. The website is a view of the archive, not the archive itself.

Forkable forever

Anyone may copy the complete archive. QEVA must remain reconstructible without qeva.org, GitHub, Vercel, or the QEVA institution.

Permanent principles

  1. Assumptions are explicit.
  2. Provenance is permanent.
  3. Formal truth is system-relative.
  4. Canonical records are immutable; corrections create descendants.
  5. The complete archive is freely reproducible.
  6. No implementation is sacred.
  7. QEVA must remain reconstructible without QEVA.
