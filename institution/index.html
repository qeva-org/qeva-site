INSTITUTION

Stewardship without ownership.

The institution maintains one canonical branch. It does not own mathematical truth and must never become necessary for access to the archive.

Constitution 0

  1. Assumptions are explicit. Nothing appears from nowhere.
  2. Provenance is permanent. Credit, revisions, objections, and refutations are not silently erased.
  3. Formal truth is system-relative. The archive distinguishes proof, conjecture, refutation, independence, and empirical evidence.
  4. Released records are immutable. Corrections create descendants.
  5. The complete archive is freely reproducible. Preservation requires no permission.
  6. No implementation is sacred. Domains, databases, theorem provers, hashes, and file formats may be replaced.
  7. QEVA must remain reconstructible without QEVA.

Institutional powers

A QEVA steward may maintain editorial standards, publish official releases, coordinate verification, fund preservation, and curate one canonical branch.

Institutional limits

A QEVA steward may not make copying contingent on membership, erase released provenance, redefine a historical record in place, or claim exclusive authority over mathematical truth.

Succession

If the steward disappears, no emergency key is required. Any mirror holding the protocol and archive can continue preservation and may publish a successor branch with explicit provenance.
