INSTITUTION

Stewardship without ownership.

A steward may curate one canonical branch. It must never become necessary to read, copy, verify, or continue the archive.

Constitution 0

  1. Assumptions are explicit. Nothing appears from nowhere.
  2. Provenance is permanent. Credit, revisions, objections, and refutations are not silently erased.
  3. Proof and semantics are distinguished. Derivability names a proof system; semantic truth names a model or semantics.
  4. Released revisions are immutable. Corrections create descendants that point backward exactly.
  5. The complete archive is freely reproducible. Preservation requires no permission.
  6. No implementation is sacred. Domains, databases, theorem provers, hashes, and file formats may be replaced.
  7. QEVA must remain reconstructible without QEVA.

Powers

A steward may publish releases, maintain editorial standards, coordinate review and formal verification, fund preservation, and curate one named canonical branch.

Limits

A steward may not erase released provenance, make archival copying contingent on membership, redefine an old revision in place, or claim exclusive authority over mathematical truth.

Succession

If the steward disappears, a successor needs no secret key to preserve the corpus. Any complete mirror may continue a successor branch, provided it preserves provenance and does not impersonate historical releases.
