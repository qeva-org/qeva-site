Recurring machinery

These are not declared to be the unique axioms of mathematics. They are recurring structural lenses whose exact scope must be stated.

Distinction & equivalenceWhen are two things the same for the observer or structure? GroupingWhat information is preserved or discarded when objects are collected into classes? Symmetry & chiralityWhich transformations preserve structure—and when does handedness survive them? Discrete & continuousHow does the topology or representation change what counts as a nearby state? RecurrenceHow can repeated rules amplify, contract, cycle, or stabilize differences? Stability & attractorsWhich perturbations decay, persist, or reorganize long-run behavior? Coarse-grainingHow can many microstates become one macrostate—and what is lost? Local & global searchWhy can exploiting nearby improvement trap a search away from a better state?

The job QEVA is trying to do

Compress the noise

Thousands of papers should resolve into a smaller graph of reusable mechanisms, claims, proofs, counterexamples, and open questions.

Expose the hidden steps

Every strong statement should make its framework, assumptions, dependencies, representations, and verification status inspectable.

Make structure executable

A concept should be simulatable when simulation is meaningful, so a visitor can perturb parameters and see what the formal words are referring to.

Show where mathematics is growing

The historical graph should reveal branches, dormant regions, unresolved bottlenecks, and bursts of new structure—without pretending popularity equals importance.

From the world to the map

  1. Ingest. Read open metadata and legally reusable text from scholarly and historical sources.
  2. Resolve. Deduplicate works, identifiers, people, concepts, and citations.
  3. Extract. Propose claims, definitions, dependencies, mechanisms, and relations.
  4. Qualify. Separate publication, conjecture, proof, formal verification, empirical evidence, refutation, and editorial interpretation.
  5. Connect. Add logical edges and historical edges as different relation types.
  6. Regenerate. Continuously rebuild the map as mathematics grows.

Rule: being published is provenance, not proof. Being popular is history, not truth.

WHY QEVA EXISTS

Do not drown in the machinery before seeing the elegant structure that generated it.

QEVA is intended to remain useful when today's frameworks, interfaces, and institutions have changed. The website is a view; the graph, protocol, provenance, and ability to regenerate the graph are the durable object.
