Axiom of choice

A simultaneous choice can be made from every member of a family of nonempty sets.

editorialset-theory · foundations

PLAIN

The idea

A simultaneous choice can be made from every member of a family of nonempty sets.

EXACT

The compact statement

One formulation: every set A of nonempty sets has a function f with domain A such that f(x)∈x for every x∈A.

EXAMPLE

For a finite family, choices can be made without the full axiom; the axiom governs arbitrary families.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Choice has many equivalent forms in ZF and consequences that may be nonconstructive; weaker and stronger variants must be named.
