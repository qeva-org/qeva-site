Axiom of infinity

At least one inductive set exists, supplying enough sets to model the natural numbers.

editorialset-theory · foundations

PLAIN

The idea

At least one inductive set exists, supplying enough sets to model the natural numbers.

EXACT

The compact statement

One formulation asserts ∃I[∅∈I ∧ ∀x(x∈I→x∪{x}∈I)].

EXAMPLE

Starting with ∅ and repeatedly adjoining x as a member yields the von Neumann finite ordinals inside I.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Infinity asserts existence; it does not by itself select one unique natural-number object.
