Collatz conjecture

The open claim that every positive integer orbit under the Collatz map eventually reaches 1.

editorialnumber-theory · dynamics · open-problems

PLAIN

The idea

Every tested starting value appears eventually to enter the 1→4→2→1 cycle, but no proof is known for all positive integers.

EXACT

The compact statement

For every n∈N_{>0}, there exists k≥0 such that T^k(n)=1.

EXAMPLE

The orbit of 6 reaches 1 after eight steps if the starting value is counted as step 0.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

This is open. Error-amplification, chaos, hierarchy, or emergent-invariance descriptions are research hypotheses unless made precise and proved.
