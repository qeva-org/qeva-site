Completeness

Completeness can mean that every semantic consequence is provable, or that a space has no missing limits; the meanings must be named.

editoriallogic · foundations

PLAIN

The idea

Completeness can mean that every semantic consequence is provable, or that a space has no missing limits; the meanings must be named.

EXACT

The compact statement

Logical completeness for a proof system S and semantics ⊨ means: if Γ⊨P, then Γ⊢ₛP. Other branches use different completeness predicates.

EXAMPLE

Gödel's completeness theorem concerns first-order logical consequence, not the completeness of every arithmetical theory.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Do not confuse logical completeness, theory completeness, metric completeness, and Gödel incompleteness.
