Constructive logic

Constructive logic treats a proof as information and does not accept every classical principle without a construction.

editoriallogic · foundations

PLAIN

The idea

Constructive logic treats a proof as information and does not accept every classical principle without a construction.

EXACT

The compact statement

In intuitionistic natural deduction, P∨¬P is not a theorem for arbitrary P; an existence proof normally carries a witness or method yielding one.

EXAMPLE

To prove ∃n P(n), one commonly gives n together with a proof of P(n).

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Constructive logic has several formulations and should not be reduced to one slogan.
