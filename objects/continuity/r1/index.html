DEFINITION · qeva:1:continuity@1

Continuity

Continuity formalizes preservation of nearness or open-set structure under a map.

editorialanalysis · topology · dynamics

PLAIN

The idea

A continuous map does not tear apart the chosen topology: inverse images of observable open regions remain open.

EXACT

The compact statement

f:X→Y is continuous iff for every open V⊆Y, f^{-1}(V) is open in X. In metric spaces this is equivalent to the ε-δ definition.

EXAMPLE

f(x)=x^2 is continuous on R with its usual topology.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Continuity is relative to the chosen topologies; a function can be continuous under one topology and not another.

ASSUMPTIONS

  • Topological spaces X and Y and a function f:X→Y are specified.

PROVENANCE
