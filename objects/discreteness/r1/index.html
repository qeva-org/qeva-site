DEFINITION · qeva:1:discreteness@1

Discrete structure

A precise notion of discreteness depends on the ambient structure; in topology, a discrete space has every singleton open.

editorialtopology · discrete-math · foundations

PLAIN

The idea

A discrete description treats states as individually separable rather than forcing arbitrarily close intermediate states.

EXACT

The compact statement

A topological space X is discrete iff every subset of X is open; equivalently, every singleton {x} is open.

EXAMPLE

The integers with the discrete topology are a discrete topological space.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

“Discrete = maximal information” is not a universal theorem. Information content depends on encoding, probability model, resolution, and task.
