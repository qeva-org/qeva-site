The idea

A graph records objects and pairwise connections while discarding most other detail.

EXACT

The compact statement

A simple undirected graph is G=(V,E) with E⊆{{u,v}:u,v∈V,u≠v}; directed and weighted graphs require extended data.

EXAMPLE

A road map can be abstracted to junction vertices and road edges.
