DEFINITION · qeva:1:grouping@2

Grouping / partition

A precise grouping can be modeled as a partition or as a map assigning objects to classes.

editorialfoundations · set-theory · information

PLAIN

The idea

Grouping makes some distinctions irrelevant by assigning multiple objects to the same class.

EXACT

The compact statement

A partition P of a set X is a family of nonempty subsets whose union is X and whose distinct members are disjoint. Equivalently, an equivalence relation ~ on X induces classes [x]={y in X:y~x}.

EXAMPLE

Integers grouped by parity form the two classes even and odd.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Not every informal act of grouping is best modeled by a partition: overlapping clusters and probabilistic memberships require other structures.

ASSUMPTIONS

  • A set X and a grouping criterion or assignment are specified.
