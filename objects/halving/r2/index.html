CONCEPT · qeva:1:halving@2

Halving

The inverse problem to doubling, defined only when the ambient structure makes division by 2 meaningful or solvable.

editorialnumber · algebra · structure

PLAIN

The idea

“Half of x” is not one universal operation. It depends on what x is and which operations exist in its mathematical structure.

EXACT

The compact statement

Given a structure with an addition operation, y is a half of x when y+y=x. A unique halving operation x↦x/2 exists only in contexts where this equation has exactly one solution y for each x under consideration.

EXAMPLE

In the real numbers, half of 7 is 7/2. In the integers, 7 has no integer half. In arithmetic modulo 5, 2 has a unique multiplicative inverse 3, so division by 2 is well-defined.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

The existence and uniqueness of halves depend on the ambient algebraic structure.
