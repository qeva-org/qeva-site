DEFINITION · qeva:1:invariant@2

Invariant

A quantity or property unchanged by a specified family of transformations.

editorialsymmetry · structure · dynamics

PLAIN

The idea

An invariant deliberately forgets changes that the chosen transformations are allowed to make.

EXACT

The compact statement

I:X→Y is invariant under G iff for every g in G and x in X, I(g·x)=I(x).

EXAMPLE

The Euclidean distance between two points is invariant under rigid motions applied to both points.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Changing the transformation family changes which quantities qualify as invariants.

ASSUMPTIONS

  • A group G acts on a set X.
  • A function I maps X to a set Y.
