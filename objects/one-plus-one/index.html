One plus one equals two

In the tiny arithmetic kernel shipped with this release, adding one to one rewrites to two.

PLAIN

The idea

In the tiny arithmetic kernel shipped with this release, adding one to one rewrites to two.

EXACT

The compact statement

With 1:=S(0), 2:=S(S(0)), add(a,0)=a, and add(a,S(b))=S(add(a,b)), add(1,1)=2 by two checked rewrites.

EXAMPLE

add(S(0),S(0)) → S(add(S(0),0)) → S(S(0)).

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

This certificate proves a statement only in the declared toy kernel; it is a demonstration of verifiable linkage, not a foundation for all arithmetic.
