DEFINITION · qeva:1:periodicity@2

Periodicity of a sequence

Exact repetition after a fixed positive index shift.

editorialdynamics · sequence · recurrence

PLAIN

The idea

A periodic sequence repeats exactly after some fixed positive number of steps.

EXACT

The compact statement

A sequence (a_n) is periodic with period p>0 iff a_{n+p}=a_n for every index n for which both terms are in the domain. A least positive such p, when it exists, is called the fundamental period.

EXAMPLE

0,1,0,1,0,1,... has period 2.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Continuous functions, group actions, tilings, and dynamical systems use related but context-specific notions of periodicity.

ASSUMPTIONS

  • A sequence (a_n) has an index domain on which n+p is defined for the stated n.
