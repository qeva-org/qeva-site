DEFINITION · qeva:1:prime@2

Prime number

A positive integer greater than 1 with no positive divisors other than 1 and itself.

editorialnumber · arithmetic

PLAIN

The idea

A prime cannot be factored into two smaller positive integers greater than 1.

EXACT

The compact statement

For p in Z with p>1, p is prime iff for every positive integer d, d divides p implies d=1 or d=p.

EXAMPLE

7 is prime. 12 is not prime because 3 divides 12 and 3 is neither 1 nor 12.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

This seed record uses the standard positive-integer definition; “prime elements” in general rings require separate contextual definitions.
