Relation

A relation records which tuples of objects stand in a chosen connection.

editorialfoundations · structure

PLAIN

The idea

A relation records which tuples of objects stand in a chosen connection.

EXACT

The compact statement

An n-ary relation on sets X₁,…,Xₙ is a subset R⊆X₁×⋯×Xₙ; writing R(x₁,…,xₙ) means the tuple belongs to R.

EXAMPLE

The less-than relation contains pairs (a,b) for which a<b.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

In type theory or categorical settings, relations may be represented differently.
