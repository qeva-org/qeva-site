Ring

PLAIN

The idea

A ring supports addition, multiplication, and additive inverses under compatible laws.

EXACT

The compact statement

A ring (R,+,·) has an abelian group under +, associative multiplication, and left/right distributivity; identity and commutativity conventions must be stated.

EXAMPLE

Integers form a commutative ring with identity.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

Authors differ on whether a ring must have 1.
