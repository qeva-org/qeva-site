Sigma-algebra

A sigma-algebra is a collection of events stable under complementation and countable combination.

editorialmeasure-theory · probability

PLAIN

The idea

A sigma-algebra is a collection of events stable under complementation and countable combination.

EXACT

The compact statement

A σ-algebra 𝓕 on Ω is a family of subsets with Ω∈𝓕, A∈𝓕⇒Ω\A∈𝓕, and A₁,A₂,…∈𝓕⇒⋃ₙAₙ∈𝓕.

EXAMPLE

The power set of a finite sample space is a sigma-algebra.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

The sigma-algebra specifies which subsets are measurable; not every subset must be admitted in every model.
