Signal and noise

Signal/noise is a task- and model-relative decomposition of observed variation.

editorialinformation · statistics · learning

PLAIN

The idea

Signal is the part treated as informative for a task; noise is the part treated as nuisance, uncertainty, or unresolved variation.

EXACT

The compact statement

A common additive model is Y=S+N, where S is the target component and N a noise component. The decomposition is meaningful only relative to assumptions about S,N and the task.

EXAMPLE

In repeated measurements, independent zero-mean measurement error may be modeled as noise around a common signal.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

The same variation can be signal for one task and noise for another; the distinction is not intrinsic to raw data alone.

ASSUMPTIONS

  • An observation model and a target inference or objective are specified.
