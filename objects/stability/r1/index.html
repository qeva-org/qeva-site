DEFINITION · qeva:1:stability@1

Stability

Stability asks whether specified behavior persists under sufficiently small perturbations.

editorialdynamics · analysis · control

PLAIN

The idea

A stable behavior does not immediately lose its qualitative or quantitative character after a small perturbation.

EXACT

The compact statement

For a fixed point x* of a map F on a metric space, Lyapunov stability means: for every ε>0 there exists δ>0 such that d(x0,x*)<δ implies d(F^n(x0),x*)<ε for all n≥0.

EXAMPLE

For F(x)=x/2 on R, x*=0 is asymptotically stable.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

There are many inequivalent notions of stability: Lyapunov, asymptotic, structural, orbital, input-output, stochastic, and others.

ASSUMPTIONS

  • A notion of perturbation, a reference behavior, and a time evolution are specified.
