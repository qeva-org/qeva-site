PLAIN

The idea

ZFC is a major foundational theory whose objects are sets governed by explicit axiom schemes and axioms.

EXACT

The compact statement

A ZFC presentation includes extensionality, empty set, pairing, union, power set, infinity, separation and replacement schemes, foundation, and choice in first-order logic with ∈.

EXAMPLE

Natural numbers, functions, spaces, and algebraic structures can be encoded as sets inside ZFC.

BOUNDARY / CAVEAT

ZFC is powerful and standard, but QEVA does not declare it the only legitimate foundation.
