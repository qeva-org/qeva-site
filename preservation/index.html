PRESERVE THE ARCHIVE · REPLACE THE WEBSITE

Longevity is a repeated social process.

No storage medium, hash algorithm, institution, or domain lasts forever unaided. QEVA survives by making loss detectable, repair ordinary, custody plural, and reconstruction boring.

INDEPENDENT CUSTODY

A mirror is not a backup owned by the same failure.

Real mirrors sit under different legal entities, administrators, funding sources, providers, regions, and media. Each can verify and serve a complete public release without central permission.

HOT MIRRORPublic HTTP copy; daily fixity and release synchronization.
COLD MIRROROffline or object-lock copy; quarterly mount and hash audit.
INSTITUTIONAL MIRRORLibrary or university custody with named succession responsibility.
PHYSICAL COREHuman-readable recovery text plus compact catalog on durable media.
FORK MIRRORAn independent line may preserve and extend the protocol without permission.

THREAT MODEL

Design for mundane failure before mythology.

BIT ROT

SHA-256 and SHA-512 manifests detect change; multiple copies provide repair material. Algorithms must be migrated before they weaken.

FORMAT ROT

Canonical UTF-8 JSONL, TSV, schemas, plain-text recovery instructions, and rendered HTML offer independent paths back.

OPERATOR FAILURE

Quarterly restore drills test whether an archive can actually be reconstructed, not merely uploaded.

INSTITUTIONAL CAPTURE

Open licensing, complete exports, signed succession records, and the permanent right to fork constrain the steward.

LEGAL OR NETWORK LOSS

Jurisdictionally diverse custodians and offline snapshots prevent one takedown or outage from becoming extinction.

SEMANTIC LOSS

The print core explains identifiers, record fields, verification levels, and the separations future readers must retain.

RELEASE RITUAL

Every public release performs the same auditable cycle.

  1. Freeze
    exact object revisions and schemas
  2. Export
    JSONL, TSV, HTML, recovery text
  3. Hash
    two digest families and BagIt manifests
  4. Replicate
    independent hot, cold, institutional copies
  5. Restore
    rebuild in a clean offline environment
  6. Witness
    publish results and unresolved failures

MILLENNIAL RULE

Never promise a million years. Preserve the ability to hand forward.

QEVA’s defensible claim is not that today’s files are immortal. It is that each generation receives explicit material, checks, rights, and duties for making the next migration.
