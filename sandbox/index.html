DISTINCTION → EQUIVALENCE → QUOTIENT

Choose which differences survive.

a ~ b ⇔ a ≡ b (mod m)

Every integer remains distinct before quotienting. After reducing modulo m, only the remainder is visible and infinitely many integers share a class.

A nearby peak is not the highest peak.
